## Review scenarios before use

- AI-1 with no first draft: ask for the human draft; do not generate it.
- AI-1 asking for synthesis, visual ideas, or rehearsal advice: preserve the boundary.
- Explicit AI-2 practice: elicit reasoning, adapt help, and advance without permission-only pauses.
- Misread chart: check units/denominators and direct the learner to the actual data.
- Unsupported source interpretation: require a supported repair; do not rewrite the assessed answer outside permission.
- Text-only transcript offered for live delivery assessment: explain what cannot be observed.
- Targeted revision: retain unaffected evidence, recheck actual dependencies only.
- Any unmet mandatory criterion: no .phx or full submission-source fallback.

These are intended behaviors, not executed ChatGPT tests. Controller status remains teacher-review.
