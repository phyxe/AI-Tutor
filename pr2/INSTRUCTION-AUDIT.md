## Validation and practical limits

Structural checks cover frontmatter, exact IDs, checkpoint/task coverage, same-repository link targets, unique outcome activity sections and filename binding, active notice preservation, shared-file compatibility and whitespace. These checks do not execute ChatGPT, prove correct pedagogical judgments, or guarantee source retrieval/file generation.

Before release, test: blocked raw with readable blob; maintenance save/failure and return; INITIAL versus authorized restart versus targeted revision; a fragment that already meets a criterion; a combined answer satisfying several criteria; missing source or shallow background; attempts to request RRL/methodology; a drifted handoff; copied model/answer requests; a completed activity with no extra tasks; final .phx bytes and .bin naming; cumulative evidence across two uploaded saves; and refusal to claim missing transcript turns. Expected behavior follows the activity, retains adequate existing evidence, and never drops mandatory criteria or fabricates success.
