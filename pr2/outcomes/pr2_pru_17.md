> RESEARCH TITLE
> The Relationship Between Regular ChatGPT Usage and Study Time Among Grade 10 – Perseverance Students
>
> RESEARCH DESIGN
> Correlational research. The study examines relationships and does not claim that ChatGPT usage causes changes in study time.
>
> CURRENT VARIABLES
> 1. ChatGPT usage frequency — number of separate school-related ChatGPT uses during the week.
> 2. ChatGPT usage duration — total time spent using ChatGPT for school-related activities.
> 3. Weekly study time — total actual self-study time outside regular class.
>
> REGULAR CHATGPT USE DEFINITION
> One separate school-related use is one opening/session of ChatGPT for one specific school task.
> - Multiple prompts within one continuous session count as 1 use.
> - Closing ChatGPT and returning later for the same or another task counts as a new use.
> Proposed eligibility criterion: at least 2 separate school-related uses on at least 2 different days within the week. This criterion is proposed and still subject to teacher/adviser approval.
>
> STUDY TIME DEFINITION
> Study time means actual self-study outside regular class.
> - Homework/review without ChatGPT counts.
> - Studying or reviewing while using ChatGPT as a tool counts.
> - Regular classroom lessons do not count.
> - ChatGPT-only answer generation/copying without actual studying or reviewing does not count.
> Example: 2 hours of actual study, including 30 minutes using ChatGPT while studying, remains 2 hours.
> If 1 hour includes only 20 minutes of actual studying and 40 minutes of answer generation/copying, record 20 minutes as study time.
>
> STUDY-TIME DATA RULES
> - Record exact daily hours for Monday–Sunday.
> - Decimal hours are preferred (example: 1 hour 30 minutes = 1.5 hours).
> - “1.30 hours” is ambiguous and should not automatically be converted.
> - If the student knows the total duration but does not remember exact start/end times, the reported duration remains valid.
> - If the student gives an uncertain estimate (e.g., “2 hours, maybe 1.5”), record as missing.
> - A blank day is missing even if the student says they studied but gives no duration.
> - A complete 7-day record is required for a weekly total.
> - If any day is missing, the weekly total is missing; do not use a partial 6-day total.
> - Explicit 0 hours is valid data, not missing.
> - If all 7 days have valid values, including zeros, sum them for the weekly total.
>
> CHATGPT DURATION RULES
> - Record daily duration for school-related ChatGPT use from Monday–Sunday.
> - Blank or unknown duration is missing.
> - Explicit 0 minutes/hours is valid zero.
> - A complete 7-day record is required for the weekly duration total.
> - If any day is missing, the weekly duration total is missing.
>
> PROPOSED RESEARCH QUESTIONS
> 1. Is there a significant relationship between the frequency of ChatGPT usage and students’ weekly study time?
> 2. Is there a significant relationship between the duration of ChatGPT usage and students’ weekly study time?
>
> PROPOSED NULL HYPOTHESES
> H₀1: There is no significant relationship between ChatGPT usage frequency and weekly study time.
> H₀2: There is no significant relationship between ChatGPT usage duration and weekly study time.
>
> ANALYSIS PLAN
> Two separate correlations will be conducted:
> 1. ChatGPT usage frequency ↔ weekly study time
> 2. ChatGPT usage duration ↔ weekly study time
> Each student’s ChatGPT measure is paired with that same student’s weekly study time.
>
> STATISTICAL CONCLUSION LOGIC
> - Significant result → reject the corresponding H₀ and conclude there is evidence of a significant relationship.
> - Not significant → fail to reject the corresponding H₀; there is not enough evidence to conclude a significant relationship.
> - Statistical significance does not establish causation.
