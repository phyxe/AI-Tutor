> ## Chapter 1 — The Research Problem and Its Background
>
> ### Final Working Title
>
> Level of Intention and Encouraging and Discouraging Factors Related to Voluntary Classroom Cleaning Among Grade 10 Students of Hawan National High School
>
> ### Background of the Study
>
> Maintaining access to safe drinking water, sanitation, and hygiene services in schools is an important part of protecting learners' health and supporting a suitable learning environment. According to the WHO/UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme (2026), 28% of schools worldwide lacked basic hygiene services in 2025, particularly access to handwashing facilities with soap and water. In the Philippines, the Department of Education (DepEd, 2016) promotes proper hygiene and sanitation practices among learners through its Water, Sanitation and Hygiene in Schools (WinS) policy. These efforts are complemented by Oplan Kalusugan sa DepEd (OK sa DepEd), which supports school health and nutrition programs (DepEd, 2018), and Brigada Eskwela, which encourages volunteerism and community involvement in supporting schools (DepEd, 2022).
>
> At Hawan National High School in Virac, Catanduanes, supporting these national health and cleanliness goals is relevant to fostering a safe and conducive learning atmosphere for students. Within this setting, the selected learners of the Grade 10–Compassion section represent a group of Junior High School students whose perspectives can offer insight into voluntary classroom cleaning. Understanding how willing these specific students are to take initiative in maintaining their own classroom environment may provide useful information about student participation in classroom cleanliness.
>
> While national school cleanliness policies exist, further local investigation may provide insight into the motivations and practical considerations related to student willingness. Specifically, this study examines Grade 10–Compassion students' intentions toward voluntary classroom cleaning and the factors they report as encouraging or discouraging their willingness. These may include environmental concern, personal health awareness, and peer cooperation as encouraging factors, as well as time constraints, availability of cleaning supplies, and workload fatigue as discouraging factors. The study also considers whether students report willingness to clean on their own initiative rather than simply following teacher instructions.
>
> This study is therefore intended to provide information about the willingness of selected Grade 10–Compassion students regarding voluntary classroom cleaning. By identifying the encouraging and discouraging factors reported by the students themselves, the findings may inform school administrators, class advisers, and student leaders at Hawan National High School as they consider supportive, student-centered approaches to voluntary classroom upkeep.
>
> ### Statement of the Problem
>
> This study aims to determine the level of intention and the encouraging and discouraging factors related to voluntary classroom cleaning among Grade 10 students of Hawan National High School.
>
> Specifically, it seeks to answer the following questions:
>
> 1. What is the level of intention of Grade 10 students to initiate classroom cleaning voluntarily without being asked?
> 2. What encouraging factors related to voluntary classroom cleaning are reported by Grade 10 students?
> 3. What discouraging factors related to voluntary classroom cleaning are reported by Grade 10 students?
>
> ### Significance of the Study
>
> This study may provide useful information about the level of intention of selected Grade 10–Compassion students at Hawan National High School to participate in voluntary classroom cleaning, as well as the encouraging and discouraging factors they report.
>
> **Grade 10–Compassion Students.** The findings may help students understand the reasons that encourage or discourage their willingness to participate in maintaining classroom cleanliness. They may also become more aware of the importance of cooperation and personal responsibility in keeping their learning environment clean.
>
> **Teachers and Class Advisers.** The results may provide teachers and class advisers with information about students' willingness and the challenges they report. This may help them consider appropriate ways to encourage student participation in classroom cleanliness.
>
> **School Administrators.** The findings may offer information that school administrators can consider when planning activities or initiatives that promote student involvement in maintaining clean and healthy learning environments.
>
> **Future Researchers.** This study may serve as a reference for future researchers who wish to conduct related studies about students' intentions, motivations, and challenges regarding voluntary classroom cleaning.
>
> ### Scope and Limitations of the Study
>
> This study focuses specifically on selected Grade 10–Compassion students at Hawan National High School. Its main purpose is to assess their level of intention to clean voluntarily and identify the specific factors they report as either encouraging or discouraging this behavior. Data for this research will be gathered strictly through survey responses rather than direct physical observation. Because of these parameters, the study only measures self-reported intentions and does not establish actual cleaning behavior or definite causal relationships. Furthermore, since the participants are limited to a single section, the findings cannot be generalized to represent the entire student population of the school.
