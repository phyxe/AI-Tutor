## Proposed title
Grade 11 Students’ Understanding of the Responsible Use of AI in School Requirements at Hawan National High School

## Background of the Study
Students can use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to help them with their school requirements, such as understanding lessons, finding information, and getting ideas for their activities. That is why it is important to know if students understand how to use AI properly and responsibly. They should not always depend on the answers it gives because some information may be incorrect or unreliable. According to Han and Gutierez (2026), students need to check if AI explanations make sense, if the sources are trustworthy, and if they understand the information themselves. Similarly, Zhou (2026) found that students compare AI-generated information with what they already know and other sources before accepting it. These studies show that using AI is not just about getting answers but also about thinking carefully and checking the information it provides. However, there is still a need to understand how Grade 11 students at Hawan National High School understand responsible AI use in their school requirements. Because of this, the study aims to explore their understanding and perspectives on using AI properly while still learning and using their own knowledge.

## Proposed research questions retained from earlier student work
**Main research question**

How do Grade 11 students from the TechPro and Academic Elective Clusters at Hawan National High School understand the responsible use of AI in doing their school requirements?

**Specific research questions**
1. How do Grade 11 students from the TechPro and Academic Elective Clusters understand the use of AI as a guide in completing their school requirements?
2. How do Grade 11 students from the TechPro and Academic Elective Clusters understand checking information from AI when doing their school requirements?
3. How do Grade 11 students from the TechPro and Academic Elective Clusters understand when and how AI should be used in completing their school requirements?
