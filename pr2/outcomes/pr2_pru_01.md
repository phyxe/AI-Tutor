### C0 — RESEARCH CONTEXT

The study focuses on the lack of a designated parking area at Hawan National High School. Vehicles, particularly motorcycles, are parked in different places, including along pedestrian pathways. This can make it difficult for students to pass through the pathways.

Learner's explanation:
“I want to know the experiences of Grade 11 students with the parking problem and whether they support having a proper parking area.”

### C1 — RESEARCH PROBLEM

The school does not have a designated parking area. Because vehicles are parked in different places, some motorcycles may occupy pedestrian pathways and make it harder for students to pass. The concern is pedestrian accessibility and having a more organized and clean school environment. The study will describe students' experiences and views; it will not claim that an accident has already happened.

### C2 — INDICATORS / INFORMATION TO GATHER

The study will gather Grade 11 students' views about:
1. Whether motorcycles parked along pedestrian pathways cause inconvenience.
2. Whether the current parking situation affects pedestrian accessibility.
3. Whether the parking situation makes the school environment less organized.
4. Whether students support establishing a proper designated parking area.

### C3 — RESPONDENTS AND ACCESS

- Target respondents: six (6) Grade 11 students.
- Sampling approach: purposive selection of Grade 11 students who have relevant experience with the current parking situation.
- Grade 11 is the target group because Grade 12 students are not allowed to be surveyed for this activity.
- Potential respondents will be approached individually and asked whether they are available. They may be offered a convenient time and comfortable place to answer.
- Participation will be voluntary. A student's decision not to participate will be respected.
- Respondents' identities will be kept private in the research report.

### C4 — RESEARCH QUESTIONS

How do Grade 11 students perceive the current parking situation at Hawan National High School?

**Specific research questions:**
