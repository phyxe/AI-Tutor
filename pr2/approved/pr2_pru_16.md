## Working Research Title

Students’ Level of Satisfaction with the Quality and Quantity of Food and Drinks Sold by Grade 12 TVL Students at Hawan National High School

## Research Context

At Hawan National High School, Grade 12 TVL students sell food and drinks to other students. The student-researcher reported hearing concerns from some buyers, including French fries that were sometimes described as oily or too soft and drinks that sometimes contained too much ice. These are initial observations and reported comments, **not verified findings about all products or buyers**.

The approved research direction is to investigate the satisfaction of student buyers rather than assume their satisfaction levels in advance. The actual satisfaction ratings of the intended respondents have not yet been established.

## Specific Research Problem

The study will describe how satisfied students who regularly purchase food or drinks sold by Grade 12 TVL students at Hawan National High School are with the **quality** and **quantity** of those products, as well as their **overall satisfaction**. Their satisfaction levels will be measured through proposed numerical ratings; no results have yet been collected.

## Main Research Question

What is the level of satisfaction of students who purchase food or drinks from Grade 12 TVL students at Hawan National High School?

## Specific Research Questions

1. What is the level of students’ satisfaction with the quality of food, in terms of taste, freshness, and texture, and the quality of drinks, in terms of taste and freshness?
2. What is the level of students’ satisfaction with the quantity of food and drinks, in terms of food serving size, amount of drink received, and amount of ice?
3. What is the students’ overall level of satisfaction with the food and drinks sold by Grade 12 TVL students?

## Main Variables and Possible Indicators

| Variable | Meaning in this study | Possible indicator/data | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Students’ level of satisfaction | Students’ reported satisfaction with the food and drinks they purchase from Grade 12 TVL students | Numerical 1–5 ratings for quality, quantity, and overall satisfaction | Main descriptive variable |
| Quality | Student buyers’ evaluations of product characteristics | Food: taste, freshness, texture; drinks: taste, freshness | Aspect of satisfaction |
| Quantity | Student buyers’ evaluations of the amount of product received | Food serving size; amount of drink received; amount of ice | Aspect of satisfaction |
| Overall satisfaction | Student buyers’ general evaluation of the purchased food and drinks | Overall 1–5 satisfaction rating | Descriptive outcome |

**Proposed five-point scale:** 1 = Very dissatisfied; 2 = Dissatisfied; 3 = Neutral; 4 = Satisfied; 5 = Very satisfied.

The five-point rating approach and these indicators were confirmed in the Activity 01 declaration. Final questionnaire wording and rating-interpretation rules are not yet approved for actual administration.

## Approved Research Type and Justification

**Quantitative Descriptive Research.**

The study intends to measure and describe students’ satisfaction with food and drink quality and quantity and their overall satisfaction, using numerical survey ratings. It does not aim to examine a statistical relationship between two separate variables or test an intervention.

**Claim boundary:** The planned results may describe respondents’ ratings. They cannot establish that product quality or quantity **causes** satisfaction or dissatisfaction.

## Intended Research Coverage

- **Setting:** Hawan National High School.
- **Products and sellers:** Food or drinks sold by Grade 12 TVL students.
- **Intended respondents:** Student buyers who purchase **either food or drinks**, not necessarily both.
- **Proposed regular-buyer criterion:** Purchases on at least **three days per school week**.
- **Proposed evidence:** Voluntary numerical satisfaction ratings, organized by product quality, product quantity, and overall satisfaction.
- **Proposed invitation method:** Grade 12 TVL students may share a survey invitation so potential respondents can independently choose whether to participate; a named buyer list is not required.
- **Study boundaries:** The focus is on satisfaction ratings of eligible student buyers, not causal proof about why they are satisfied or dissatisfied, and not unmeasured opinions attributed to all school students.
- **Potential limitations:** Voluntary participation and self-reported ratings may affect who responds and how experiences are evaluated.
