## Working Research Title

Tool Return and Storage Practices During Selected Grade 12 TVL Workshop Sessions

## Research Context

During workshop activities, learners use tools that should be returned and organized according to the designated storage arrangements. Whether and how consistently these practices occur across selected sessions is an empirical question, not an established finding. This fictional proposed study focuses on observable tool-return and storage practices rather than assumptions about individual learners' attitudes or responsibility.

## Specific Research Problem

The proposed study seeks to describe how consistently tools used in selected Grade 12 TVL workshop sessions are returned to their designated places and organized according to the workshop's storage guide. The actual extent of these practices has not been established by this sample record.

## Main Research Question

What are the observed tool-return and storage practices during selected Grade 12 TVL workshop sessions?

## Specific Research Questions

1. What proportion of tools used in the selected workshop sessions are returned to their designated places by the end of each session?
2. What proportion of the used tools are arranged according to the designated storage guide by the end of each session?
3. How do the observed proportions vary across the selected workshop sessions?

## Main Measured Variables and Possible Indicators

| Measured feature | Meaning in this study | Possible indicator/data |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tool-return practice | Whether a tool used in a selected session is returned to its designated place at the observation point | Number returned divided by the number of used tools, recorded per session |
| Tool-organization practice | Whether a used tool is arranged according to the applicable workshop storage guide | Number correctly arranged divided by the number of used tools, recorded per session |

The tool/session is the observation unit; the study need not collect student names or assign individual blame.

## Proposed Quantitative Research Type

**Quantitative descriptive.** The study aims to count and describe the observed proportions across selected workshop sessions. It does not attempt to determine causes or prove the effectiveness of an intervention.
