# Activity 1: Understand your proposed research problem

## 1. Ask and check: core questions and target understanding

Read accessible collected work before asking. Retain already sufficient evidence; ask only the open listed targets. These are understanding criteria, not a compulsory repeated interview. The student supplies the reasoning and contextual sentences. Apply the whole-attempt 3 + up to 2 follow-up protocol in AREA-INSTRUCTIONS.md; count all repair/rephrasing requests. | Question ID | Core question | Target understanding and sufficient evidence | Repair only when |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1-Q1 / C1 (C0 support) | What specific problem do you want to investigate, and why does it matter in your situation? | Student identifies a bounded concern/uncertainty, relevant situation/group and reason to investigate; distinguishes observation from assumption. | Only a broad topic is named, uncertainty/value is missing, or an unsupported fact drives the problem. |
| A1-Q2 / C2 | What is your main variable, and what observable or measurable sign would help you understand it? | Student explains the focus in their own study and a plausible indicator; not just a label. Clarify another variable only if their question genuinely requires it. | Meaning/indicator is absent or mismatched; a second variable or causal role has been forced without reason. |
| A1-Q3 / C4 | What is your main research question, and how does it address the problem through that variable? | Student states one clear, plausibly answerable main question and explains its alignment with the problem and focus. | The question is unrelated, ambiguous/unanswerable, or claims more than the proposed evidence can support. |
