| A3-Q6 / M7–M8 | How would your planned coding and analysis answer the questions, and which conclusions would go beyond that evidence? | Question → indicator/item → unit/code/score → suitable proposed descriptive or justified bivariate analysis → claim limit is traceable; student understands conditions rather than merely naming a test. | A test is forced onto a descriptive question, coding/measurement/analysis conflict, or findings/causal/general claims are invented. |
