## Important adaptations

- Planning, learner understanding, actual document/evidence completeness and real-world clearance are distinct. A short session may submit partial work; gaps are not filled or labeled completed.
- Three standard follow-ups plus at most two justified extras apply across all targets/items/sources, with same-attempt continuity. No additional instrument-item/source interview loops.
- The tutor explains errors and guides student choices without writing the exact instrument, methodology or rationale for approval/copying. Retain original attempts, existing drafts and real assistance.
- Minimum literature/authoritative measurement support, working definitions and provisional variable relationships precede any claimed instrument lock; a full RRL/framework/hypothesis assignment is deferred to a separately assigned stage.
- Quality checks and permissions use genuine evidence/status only. No invented reviewer, date, signature, pilot participant, coefficient or approval. A coefficient/signature alone is not validity proof.
- Actual teacher clearance for an exact protocol/version may be recorded, never issued or extended by the tutor. Pending access/consent/pilot requirements do not prevent collecting the proposed package for review, but block the real-world action.
- A locked-question/instrument alternative stays a proposal with baseline and reason, not a silent change. Independently authorized planning can continue. Real conflicting/absent entry authority is diagnosed precisely.
- No names, respondent-level identifiable records or private consent forms are required in a public/private tutor upload; use privacy-safe summaries or protected teacher locations.

## Editorial scenario checks

1. A teacher routes a student with Chapter 1 clarification: map it to an A3 target and submit baseline/clarification plus proposal without another whole Chapter 1 approval cycle.
2. A reference says earlier stages are complete but the actual student record does not: use current official route/baseline; do not infer approval from the reference.
3. An instrument lacks real review/pilot evidence: record pending; collect the draft and meaning/quality understanding within budget; never tick Cleared or advise participant action.
4. A convenience sample is called random, a form called a design, or a descriptive question forced into significance testing: explain the precise issue and elicit student correction within the shared allowance.
5. An exact item is available: inspect its real indicator/response meaning/code/analysis link; do not invent missing items or run a full extra oral defense for every item.
6. The follow-up allowance ends: stop repairs, collect genuinely unasked main prompts, then submit honest complete/incomplete evidence. No automatic mastery or new chat reset.
7. File tools fail: deliver the authorized structured chat report, no fake download or manual custom-file assembly. Save-only/maintenance retains its actual stop rules.
8. Literature later contradicts a locked/used instrument: preserve versions, identify the consequence for teacher review, never silently recode/reinterpret already collected responses.
9. The proposed package is coherent but fieldwork authorization is pending: record those separate states and stop for the teacher; do not recruit, pilot or access restricted records.
10. Student asks for Chapter 2/4 next: do not start an unassigned tutor activity. Future literature work needs its own teacher assignment; missing collection permission does not allow unauthorized data gathering.
